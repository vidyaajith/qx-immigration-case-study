The Challenge

The founder was the practice.

Every case moved through him. Every hire, every fire, every judgment call. There was no standard way to handle a case, no fixed path for a new client to enter the practice, and no way to see what was happening across the team without asking. The practice could not run for a week without him in it.

My Approach

Before automating anything, the practice's actual case flow had to be mapped end to end, and the team had to be rebuilt around people who fit where it was headed.

01

Audit the Case Flow

Mapped every step from first client contact through case resolution, including where founder involvement was actually required versus habitual.

02

Rebuild the Team

Let go of team members who didn't align with the direction of the practice, and hired case managers who did.

03

Build the Operating System

Stood up the project management structure, case handling standards, and central visibility layer the team now runs on.

04

Automate and Interconnect

Connected the case management, intake, and reporting tools with AI-powered workflows so repeatable work ran without manual handling.

The Leak

The practice wasn't short on clients, it was short on a system that could run their cases without the founder standing in the middle of every one.

The Audit

Four operational gaps.

Mapping the case flow end to end, from first client contact through case resolution, surfaced exactly where the practice was leaking time, and why growth had stalled.

  • No standardized case handling. Case quality and speed depended on which team member picked it up, not on a defined process.
  • No central visibility. Case status, intake status, and team workload lived in separate tools nobody could see end to end.
  • Hiring wasn't aligned to the vision. The team in place did not match where the practice needed to go, so growth stalled instead of compounding.
  • Intake had no fixed path. New clients entered the practice however they happened to arrive, so the founder had to personally shepherd the ones that mattered.

The Build

Four moves, one operating system.

01

The Case Operating System

One structure for every case, not a mental model per case manager.

Built the practice's project management workspace from scratch in ClickUp, with a customized workflow for each case type, O-1 and beyond. Every case now moves through the same defined stages regardless of which case manager owns it, and nothing lives only in someone's head.

One board holds every active case, not memory
  • Customized case handling. Each case type moves through its own defined stage sequence instead of an improvised one.
  • Central visibility. Case status, intake status, and workload now live in one place anyone can check.
02

Intake, Standardized

New clients stopped waiting on the founder to notice them.

Built a defined intake workflow from first contact through case assignment, so a new client's path into the practice stopped depending on who happened to see the message first, and the founder stopped being the default point of triage.
